Safety Guidelines

Practical safety rules for respectful, strictly platonic meetups.

Meet in public

Choose a busy, well-lit public venue for the first meeting. Do not agree to a private home, hotel room or isolated location. Arrange your own transport.

Tell someone

Share venue and timing with a trusted person, keep your phone charged and leave if uncomfortable.

Keep it platonic

Dating, sexual solicitation, escort services, harassment, coercion and off-platform payment requests are prohibited.

Protect your information

Never share OTPs, passwords, UPI PINs, banking details or identity documents in chat.

Report and emergency

Use in-product report/block controls where available or email support@yorbuddy.com. Email is not an emergency service. If in immediate danger in India, move to safety and call 112. YorBuddy does not promise live SOS monitoring or 24/7 incident response.

Enforcement

Reports may be investigated and accounts restricted or suspended where appropriate. Do not rely on a verification badge as a guarantee of safety.
