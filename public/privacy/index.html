Privacy Policy

How YorBuddy uses and protects personal information.

Information collected

Depending on features used, we may process account details, contact information, profile preferences, messages, support reports, booking and payment references, device/log data and verification information where that check is enabled.

Purposes

We use information to provide accounts, matching, bookings, payments, safety and support; prevent fraud and abuse; comply with law; and improve the service. Where consent is required, we will request it before the relevant processing and explain the purpose. Consent withdrawal requests can be sent to support@yorbuddy.com, subject to lawful retention.

Sharing and retention

Information may be shared with hosting, communications, identity-verification and payment providers, with other members as needed for platform features, or with authorities where legally required. We do not sell personal information. We retain data only as reasonably necessary for stated purposes, security, disputes and legal obligations.

Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access, correction or erasure, withdraw consent, raise a grievance and exercise other rights available under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and rules in force. Contact support@yorbuddy.com. We may verify your identity before acting.

Children and cookies

YorBuddy is 18+ and does not knowingly provide accounts to children. Essential storage may support account and security features. If non-essential analytics or advertising are enabled, notice and consent controls must be presented before activation.

Grievance

The Grievance Officer has not yet been formally designated in the records available for this staging build. The officer's verified name and contact details must be published before production launch. This draft requires legal review.

Last updated: 20 November 2026. This staging policy text requires qualified Indian legal review before production publication.
