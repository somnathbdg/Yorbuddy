How to plan a buddy activity

  1. Choose the activity and check which options are currently available.
  2. Review profile details and the verification information displayed by YorBuddy.
  3. Agree on the venue, timing, costs and expectations before meeting.

YorBuddy is strictly platonic and is not a dating or escort service. Verification status is limited to the checks described by the platform and does not guarantee safety or conduct. For first meetings, choose a busy public place, tell someone you trust and arrange your own transport. Read the safety guidelines.

Questions about activity buddies

Does YorBuddy guarantee that I will find a buddy?

No. Connections and availability depend on active members, location and the activity selected.

Is YorBuddy only for one kind of activity?

The platform describes use cases such as coffee, movies, shopping, events, conversations and city exploration. Check the current app for supported options.

Do I need to be an adult?

Yes. YorBuddy is for adults aged 18 and above.
