Frequently Asked Questions

Answers about age, verification, pricing, access passes and safety.

Who can use YorBuddy?

Adults aged 18 or older only.

Is it dating or escort?

No. YorBuddy is strictly platonic. Dating, sexual services and escort solicitation are prohibited.

What does verified mean?

It means only that the specific checks shown in an account status have been completed and approved. It is not a guarantee of safety or conduct.

What does the one-day pass provide?

It provides the platform features and access duration stated on the checkout screen. It does not guarantee a buddy or booking. Confirm the amount and validity before paying.

Payment issue?

If your account is not activated after a debit or you see a duplicate charge, email support@yorbuddy.com with the transaction reference.

How should we meet?

Meet first in a busy public place, tell a trusted person and arrange your own transport. Call 112 in an emergency.
