Make Friends in India Through Shared Activities

Making a new connection can start with a simple plan: coffee, a movie, shopping, a local event or exploring a city. YorBuddy helps adults discover platonic activity companionship for the moments they would rather not do alone.

Find company for the things you enjoy

Choose the kind of activity you have in mind and explore the people and options available on the platform. YorBuddy is designed around shared experiences and respectful, strictly platonic connections—not dating or escort services.

  • Coffee and conversations: plan a relaxed catch-up at a public café.
  • Movies: find company for a film outing.
  • Shopping: make a shopping trip a shared plan.
  • Events: explore public events and local experiences.
  • City outings: discover places and activities around your city.

How YorBuddy works

  1. Explore YorBuddy and choose the activity or city that interests you.
  2. Review available profile and verification information before deciding to connect.
  3. Discuss the plan and expectations clearly before meeting.

Member availability varies by location and time. A profile or platform verification status is not a guarantee of a person's identity, conduct or safety. Read the YorBuddy safety guidelines before arranging an in-person meeting.
