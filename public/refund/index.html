Refund & Cancellation Policy

Information about digital access passes, cancellations and payment support.

Before payment

Review the access duration, features and amount shown at checkout. A one-day pass provides access for the stated period and does not guarantee a booking or companion availability.

Member cancellations

Because digital access may begin immediately after payment, a pass is generally non-cancellable after activation, except where required by law or where a payment is duplicated, unauthorised or the service was not delivered as described.

Failed or duplicate payments

If money was debited but access was not activated, or you were charged twice, contact support@yorbuddy.com with registered email, transaction reference, date and amount. Never send card details, OTPs or UPI PINs.

Refund processing

Eligible refunds, once approved, will be initiated to the original payment method through the payment provider. Bank/network timelines may vary. If YorBuddy cancels a paid service or cannot provide purchased access, an appropriate remedy will be assessed under applicable law. Final timelines and statutory rights require legal/payment-provider review before launch.
